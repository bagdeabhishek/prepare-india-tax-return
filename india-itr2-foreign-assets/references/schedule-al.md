# Schedule AL

## Contents

1. Applicability and date
2. Immovable assets
3. Bank deposits
4. Shares and securities
5. Other movable assets
6. Related liabilities
7. Joint ownership
8. Controls

## 1. Applicability and date

Verify the current AY form, instructions, and validation rules. For AY 2026-27,
the official ITR-2 manual and validation rules require Schedule AL when total
income exceeds ₹1 crore. Do not carry that threshold into another AY without
verification.

Schedule AL is a 31 March statement. Schedule FA is generally a calendar-year
disclosure ending 31 December. Never copy FA closing values into AL.

Use the field's required valuation basis. When the screen says `Amount (cost)`,
report supported historical cost, not current market value.

## 2. Immovable assets

Create one row per supportable asset interest with:

- Description and complete address.
- Total supported acquisition/construction cost accumulated through 31 March.
- Taxpayer's ownership share of that cost.
- Source invoice/payment rows and their dates.

For an under-construction property, total only amounts actually paid or otherwise
forming supported cost through 31 March. Do not include instalments invoiced or
paid after the reporting date. Keep stamp duty and other acquisition-linked
items separately identified so their inclusion follows the current valuation
instructions; do not bury ambiguous club, maintenance, financing, or service
charges in property cost.

Do not use the loan sanctioned amount, current builder demand, market value, or
future instalments as cost.

## 3. Bank deposits

`Bank (including all deposits)` should not be zero merely because ordinary
savings balances are small. Build a 31 March bank ledger containing:

- Savings/current balances.
- Fixed/term/recurring deposits.
- Supported foreign cash/depository balances translated under the applicable AL
  instructions.
- Taxpayer's share of joint accounts.

Do not use interest income as the deposit balance. Reconcile bank totals to
31 March statements or certificates.

## 4. Shares and securities

Include domestic and foreign shares/securities owned at 31 March using the
current AL cost basis. For employee equity:

- Use lot-level historical acquisition cost supported by vest/ESPP documents.
- Do not enter zero merely because shares were granted by an employer.
- Do not substitute 31 March market value when the field asks for cost.
- Exclude shares actually disposed of before 31 March.

Reconcile lot quantities and cost to the foreign-equity ledger. Schedule FA A3
values can be a useful cross-check but use different dates/conversion mechanics
and are not copied into AL.

## 5. Other movable assets

Review every displayed category:

- Jewellery/bullion.
- Art/collections.
- Vehicles, yachts, boats, aircraft.
- Insurance policies.
- Loans and advances given.
- Cash in hand.

Zero is acceptable only after a factual review. Record the source or taxpayer
confirmation supporting each material zero.

## 6. Related liabilities

Report only liabilities incurred in relation to assets included in Schedule AL.
Use outstanding principal at 31 March from the lender statement/certificate, not
the original sanction, total EMI, interest paid, or a later-date balance.

Maintain:

`asset row → related loan account → 31 March principal → taxpayer share`

Do not net the liability against asset cost; report asset and liability in their
separate fields.

## 7. Joint ownership

Determine ownership from the sale agreement/title and liability from the loan
agreement or other evidence. Apply the taxpayer's supported share independently
to:

- Property cost.
- Joint bank balances.
- Shares/securities, if jointly held.
- Related outstanding principal.

Equal co-borrowers do not automatically prove equal beneficial ownership.
Document any 50% assumption and replace it when stronger evidence is available.

## 8. Controls

- Reporting date is 31 March.
- Total income was tested against the current AL threshold.
- Immovable cost ties to dated payments through 31 March.
- Bank total includes fixed deposits and joint-account share.
- Shares/securities includes foreign employee shares at supported cost.
- No 31 December FA market value was copied into AL.
- Liability is outstanding principal related to listed assets.
- Joint ownership/liability percentages are evidenced.
- Every non-zero figure has source/page provenance.
