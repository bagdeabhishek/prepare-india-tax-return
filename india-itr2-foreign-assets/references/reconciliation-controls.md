# Reconciliation controls

## Contents

1. Document controls
2. Share controls
3. Cash and income controls
4. Schedule controls
5. CSV controls
6. Final review

## 1. Document controls

- Every source file is inventoried.
- Duplicate/overlapping statements are labelled.
- Statement periods cover the complete required financial and calendar years.
- Password-protected files were opened without persisting credentials.
- Each material figure has document/page provenance.

## 2. Share controls

- Opening shares + acquired - sold/transferred = closing shares.
- Gross vested - tax withheld = net delivered.
- Sum of A3 closing lot shares = issuer closing shares.
- Sum of issuer holdings = custodial securities total.
- Actual sales have trade confirmation, proceeds, date, and quantity.
- Tax-withheld shares are not misclassified as capital-gain sales.

## 3. Cash and income controls

- Quarterly cash flows tie opening to closing cash.
- Dividends tie shares and declared per-share amounts.
- Detailed foreign tax ties withholding transactions.
- Differences from rounded 1042-S/annual forms are explained.
- A1/A2/A3 calendar credits are not added multiple times to taxable income.

## 4. Schedule controls

- Schedule OS foreign income = Schedule FSI foreign income by head, subject to documented mechanics.
- FSI foreign tax = TR foreign tax = Form 67 foreign tax.
- TR relief = FSI relief and does not exceed the cap.
- Dividend accrual buckets sum to Schedule OS dividend.
- A2 closing = A3 securities closing + foreign cash/other account assets.
- A3 proceeds reconcile to actual foreign sales and Schedule CG.
- Part B-TI equals salary + HP + gains + other sources after setoff/deductions.
- Dependent CYLA/BFLA/CFL/SI/TI/TTI schedules were refreshed.
- Schedule AL uses 31 March, not 31 December.
- Schedule IT equals the actual self-assessment challan.
- Part B-TTI includes the challan and shows zero payable.
- The final utility export has a regenerated digest.
- Form 67 acknowledgement matches the assessment year and claimed FTC.

## 5. CSV controls

- Correct current template/header retained.
- Operational field count confirmed with a parser.
- No hidden BOM/non-breaking spaces unless accepted.
- No unexpected trailing field or empty row.
- Country codes and dropdown labels match current UI.
- Dates retain literal format after spreadsheet export.
- A one-row import test succeeded.
- Imported row count and totals match the source.

## 6. Final review

For every material amount classify:

- Exact.
- Rounded from official tax form.
- Converted with documented rate.
- Derived/control total.
- Proxy requiring replacement.
- Unresolved.

Do not call the return complete while a required material figure remains unresolved.
