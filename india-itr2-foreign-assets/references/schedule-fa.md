## 1. Scope and period

Verify current applicability based on residential status. For the relevant assessment year, Schedule FA generally asks for assets/accounts held at any time during the calendar year ending 31 December.

Do not substitute the Indian financial year for Schedule FA’s calendar-year window.

## 2. A1 — Foreign depository accounts

Typical fields:

- Country/code.
- Depository institution name/address/ZIP.
- Account number.
- Status.
- Opening date.
- Peak balance.
- 31 December closing balance.
- Gross calendar-year interest.

A broker’s bank-deposit or cash-sweep program can create an A1 disclosure even when the overall brokerage account is disclosed in A2.

## 3. A2 — Foreign custodial accounts

Typical fields:

- Custodian identity and account.
- Owner/beneficial-owner/beneficiary status.
- Opening date.
- Peak total account value.
- Closing total account value.
- Gross credits by nature: interest, dividend, sale/redemption proceeds, or other income.

Reconcile:

`A2 closing = securities closing + cash/depository closing + other custodial assets`

When one account has several credit natures, use multiple supported detail lines if the UI permits. If the portal only accepts one nature, retain a precise component workpaper and use the current portal-supported aggregate/manual method.

## 4. A3 — Foreign equity and debt interests

Enter an issuer/lot row with:

- Country/code.
- Entity name/address/ZIP.
- Nature of entity.
- Acquisition date.
- Initial investment value.
- Peak value while held.
- Closing value.
- Gross amount paid/credited with respect to the holding.
- Gross sale/redemption proceeds.

Use separate acquisition lots when needed to preserve acquisition value, holding-period peak, closing value, or income allocation.

## 5. Overlap

A1, A2, and A3 may disclose different legal/economic views of related property:

- A1: foreign depository/cash account.
- A2: broker/custodial account.
- A3: underlying foreign equity/debt interests.

This disclosure overlap does not authorize counting the same income multiple times in Schedule OS.

## 6. Currency conversion

Follow current official Schedule FA instructions for SBI telegraphic transfer buying rate and relevant dates.

Maintain separate calculations for:

- A1/A2 peak date.
- 31 December closing date.
- A3 acquisition date.
- A3 peak date during each lot’s holding period.
- Gross income or proceeds using the date required by the current instructions.

Do not use market value as acquisition value, acquisition value as closing value, or an annual average rate without authority.

## 7. Schedule AL

If Schedule AL applies, report assets as of 31 March using the current Schedule AL valuation instructions. Schedule FA disclosure does not replace AL.

Do not copy 31 December FA values into 31 March AL. Use the statement covering 31 March and include foreign securities/cash in the appropriate AL categories.

## 8. Assumptions

If formal account-opening date is unavailable:

- Search account profile, first statement, welcome letter, and prior filed return.
- If still unavailable, use an explicitly labelled earliest-documented-activity proxy only after explaining it.
- Keep a replacement instruction for when the actual date is found.

Apply the same disclosure discipline to missing acquisition dates or incomplete cost data. Never disguise a proxy as exact.
