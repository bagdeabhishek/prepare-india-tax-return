## 1. Document types

Use:

- Quarterly account statements for opening/closing shares, cash, dividends, withholding, and dated deposits.
- Annual vest reports for gross vested shares, withheld shares, net delivered shares, and vest FMV.
- Individual vest notices for jurisdiction and tax-withholding method.
- Trade confirmations for actual market sales.
- Employer Form 16/payroll records for perquisite taxation.

Do not label a document by filename alone. A file named “Sale” can be a vest/withholding notice.

## 2. RSUs

For each vest:

- Gross shares vested.
- Shares withheld for tax.
- Net shares delivered.
- Vest date.
- Vest-date FMV per share.
- Tax jurisdiction.
- Whether broker states “shares sold” or merely “shares withheld.”

Normally:

`gross vested - withheld = net delivered`

For Schedule FA A3 acquisition value, use the supportable vest-date value of the acquired interest and the applicable Schedule FA conversion rate. Retain the employer-perquisite reconciliation separately.

## 3. ESPP

Capture:

- Subscription/offering FMV.
- Purchase-date FMV.
- Employee purchase price.
- Shares acquired.
- Discount included in salary/perquisites.

Do not automatically use employee purchase price for every tax field. Determine whether the field calls for investment cost, taxable FMV, or capital-gain cost basis under the applicable rule.

## 4. Tax-withheld shares

Shares withheld at vest for Indian payroll tax are not automatically a capital-gain sale.

Treat as a disposal only when the evidence shows an actual sale, sale proceeds, execution price, and sale date. A vest notice’s “gross proceeds” may merely mean vest FMV used to calculate payroll withholding.

Document:

- Withholding method.
- Number withheld.
- Jurisdiction.
- Whether open-market sale occurred.
- Whether proceeds were remitted to payroll/tax authority.

## 5. Lot construction

Create one A3 lot row per acquisition date when quantities, acquisition values, peak values, or income allocations differ.

For each lot:

- Determine shares remaining during the calendar year.
- Determine acquisition value.
- Determine maximum holding value while the lot was held.
- Determine 31 December closing value for shares still held.
- Allocate dividends to shares held on each record date/payment date, consistently with the instructions used.
- Record zero sale proceeds when no actual sale/redemption occurred.

## 6. Sales

For actual foreign-share sales:

- Use trade confirmations.
- Separate sale proceeds and cost conversions under the applicable capital-gain rules.
- Determine holding period under Indian law.
- Do not use section 112A merely because the foreign company is listed abroad.
- Reconcile Schedule CG proceeds to A3 sale/redemption proceeds, allowing different conversion rules when applicable.

## 7. Controls

- Sum of open lots = broker closing quantity.
- Sum of lot closing values = issuer closing holding value.
- Issuer holding value + account cash = custodial closing balance.
- Annual vest gross, withheld, and net shares tie to quarterly deposits.
- Dividend per share × eligible shares ties to cash credits.
- Any difference between exact statements and rounded tax forms is explicitly explained.
